# Counters: numbers above 10

Status: design, 2026-10-26, for the owner's approval. The owner skipped the question round, so every
choice here is mine and marked as such; the decisions the owner must make are listed at the end. Nothing is
built until this is approved. The composition rules in the appendix need a teacher's check before any data
that uses them ships.

## Problem

Counters are taught for 1 to 10 and 何 only, and the original design said so on purpose: all the sound
irregularity lives there, and the app never composes a reading. The cost is that the phrases learners say
most are outside it: 15分, 30分, 45分, 11時 to 24時, 11日 to 31日 and 20日 (はつか), 20歳 (はたち), 20本,
100円, 1000円 and the months 十一月 and 十二月. These are where the interesting sound changes continue
(三十分 さんじゅっぷん, 二十本 にじゅっぽん, 三百本 さんびゃっぽん) and where a learner has nothing to look up.

## Goals

- Every regular counter has readings for 11 to 99, and a few round numbers beyond, **generated by the data
  pipeline and validated**, like the 1 to 10 readings. The app still derives nothing.
- A learner can look up, hear and be quizzed on a bigger number as easily as 1 to 10, and find it by
  searching (15分, 十五分, じゅうごふん).
- The numbers each counter really needs are covered, not a blanket range: 時 to 24, 日 to 31, 分 to 59,
  円 up to ten thousand.
- A month-of-the-year counter (月, 1 to 12), the gap left by milestone 4.

## Non-goals

- Years (二〇二六年), numbers above 99 except 100 and 円's round numbers, 万 and 億 compounds, decimals,
  fractions, ordinals (番目 stays as it is).
- Changing how 1 to 10 and 何 look and work. The current screens must keep working with their data.
- Pitch accent and audio.

## Anti-goals

- A rule engine in the app. If readings could be computed on the device the data would not need to be
  checked, and a wrong rule would ship in a release instead of a data update.
- Hand-writing 2,400 readings. Regular counters are generated; only the irregular ones are authored.
- A bigger data file that slows launch or search. The budget is below.

## Approach

### Data: more keys, the same shape

`readings` is already a map from a key to readings, with `"1"` to `"10"` and `"what"`. Extended numbers are
more keys (`"15"`, `"20"`, `"100"`), with the preferred reading first and `changed` and `distractors` defined
exactly as today. No new structure, so old app builds ignore the extra keys and the 1 to 10 screens do not
change.

Two optional per-counter fields say which numbers a counter has:

- `extent` (a whole number from 10 to 99, default 10, so a counter opts in): the largest number composed for it. 時 is 24, 日 (date) 31, 分 59,
  月 12. A counter that cannot be counted past ten (つ) has `extent` 10.
- `extra`: a list of round numbers beyond `extent` that are generated or authored too: `100` for most counters,
  and for 円 `100` to `900`, `1000` to `9000` and `10000`.

### Generation: rules for regular counters, tables for irregular ones

`update_data.py` composes a reading for each number from the counter's own 1 to 10 readings, using the rules
in the appendix:

- a tens prefix (にじゅう, さんじゅう …) plus the counter's reading for the last digit, so 十四本 is
  じゅうよんほん and 十四時 じゅうよじ follow from 時's own 4;
- a round ten takes the counter's reading for 10 with the prefix in front, so the same small っ that makes
  じゅっぽん makes さんじゅっぽん (and にじっぽん is kept as an accepted alternative);
- 100 takes ひゃっ before か and は row counters (ひゃっぽん, ひゃっこ) and ひゃく otherwise.

Irregular counters compose through a small `compound` field: the digits that read differently inside a
compound (人: 1 is いちにん and 2 ににん, so 十一人 is じゅういちにん, not じゅうひとり) and the reading after a
round ten (人: にん, 日: にち). Counters that name numbers irregularly (日: 十一日 じゅういちにち, 十四日 じゅうよっか, 二十日 はつか, 十七日 じゅうしちにち; 歳: 二十歳
はたち) author the keys that differ in `overrides`, as they author 1 to 10 today. For the two 日 counters and
日間 that means authoring 11 to 31. Everything an override does not cover is composed.

`changed` marks a number whose preferred reading is not the plain composition (二十本 にじゅっぽん is not
にじゅうほん), so the tint and the Sound patterns screen carry over. `distractors` are generated for extended
numbers the same way as for 1 to 10.

Validation, in the same pipeline: for every class (plain, k, s, h-b, h-p) one counter's generated table at the
numbers 11, 14, 18, 20, 30, 40, 100 is compared with a hand-written expected table (as the 1 to 10 classes
are today); every composed reading must be hiragana and non-empty; irregular counters must override every key
their `extent` asks for; and the counted-run check understands compound numerals (十五分, 二十歳, 三百円),
which today it skips.

### Size

counters.json was 64 KB. About 2,700 extra reading lists, `changed` entries and distractors (only for an
extended number that changed or is authored; every other key has an empty list) bring it to about 199 KB,
within a 200,000-byte budget a test pins. My first estimate, 150 KB, was low. One sync, parsed once. Search over about 3,000
counted forms is a linear scan of a few thousand short strings.

### App

- **Model.** `CounterNumber.n(Int)` already holds any integer. It gains kanji for any number (十五, 二十, 百)
  and keeps `allCases` as the core 1 to 10 and 何, which the strip, tables, widget and Sound patterns use, so
  none of them changes. `Counter.readings(for:)` returns the extended readings when they exist and nothing
  when they do not.
- **Counter page.** Below the 1 to 10 strip, a small stepper: **− 10, − 1, the number, + 1, + 10**, starting at
  11 and limited to the counter's `extent` and `extra`. The reading block already shows whatever number is
  selected, so 十五分 shows じゅうごふん with the same tint when it changed. No keyboard, no sheet.
- **Compare tables.** A segmented control above the table: **1–10**, **11–20**, **30–90**, **100+**, showing only
  the ranges the section's counters have. The table keeps its 44 pt rows, so each range fits one screen.
- **Search.** `kanjiNumerals` turns 1 to 99 and 100 into kanji (15分 finds 十五分); the forms search covers
  the extended keys; the noisy-prefix rule is extended so じゅう alone matches nothing.
- **Quiz.** *Read it* keeps drawing from 1 to 10 and 何. A new quiz row, **Bigger numbers**, asks *Read it* for
  extended numbers, weighted to the ones that changed or are irregular (二十歳, 三十分, 三百円). Mixed
  includes a sample. Weak spots already keys by counter and number, so they work unchanged.
- **Examples and pitfalls** for the numbers learners meet first: 二十歳 はたち, 二十日 はつか, 三十分
  さんじゅっぷん, 四十分 and 十四日 (よんじゅっぷん, じゅうよっか), 百円 ひゃくえん, and 十二月 じゅうにがつ.
- **月.** A new counter `gatsu` (がつ) with 四月 しがつ, 七月 しちがつ, 九月 くがつ authored and `extent` 12.

### Delivery in three stages, each shipping alone

1. **Data and pipeline:** composition rules, `extent` and `extra`, the validator and its tests, the compound
   numeral check, the 月 counter, all generated tables, the real-data tests. Needs the teacher's check of the
   appendix before the data merges, and the owner's signature.
2. **App:** the model, the stepper, the Compare ranges, search. Works without stage 3.
3. **Quiz and content:** Bigger numbers, the examples and pitfalls above.

## Constraints

- The owner re-signs every `data/` change, on the feature branch; furigana for each new counted run in
  examples is authored with the content (the pipeline already demands it).
- Old caches and old builds must keep working: the new fields and keys are optional.
- iPad, macOS, Android and watchOS keep compiling; their screens are not changed here.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Scope** (my default): 11 to 99 for regular counters, with `extent` trimmed to what each really needs, plus
   `100` and 円's round numbers. Smaller would be tens only (20, 30 … 90, 100); larger starts to need 千 and
   万 rules. *Recommended: the default.*
2. **Generated or authored.** Generated for regular counters, authored only for 人, 日, 日間, 歳 and 月.
   The alternative, authoring every table, is about 2,400 entries to write and review.
   *Recommended: generated, because the pipeline can then prove it.*
3. **Stepper or typed number** on the counter page. The stepper needs no keyboard and cannot ask for a number
   the data does not have; a typed field is faster for 45. *Recommended: the stepper.*
4. **Alternates.** Keep accepted alternates in compounds (にじっぽん, じゅうはちほん) as with 1 to 10, or only the
   preferred reading? *Recommended: keep them, shown small as now.*
5. **Teacher gate.** The appendix is reviewed before stage 1 merges. Without it, I would not publish the data.
   *Dropped by the owner on 2026-10-27:* the numbers ship without waiting, and every number above 10 is
   marked "unreviewed" in the app, with the same small label the Genki lesson mappings use.

## Appendix: composition rules to be checked by a teacher

Let `prefix(t)` be に, さん, よん, ご, ろく, なな, はち, きゅう for 2 to 9 tens, and nothing for 10.
For a counter with `reading` R and its own readings `r(d)` for d = 1 to 10:

| Case | Reading | Example |
|---|---|---|
| tens + ones, ones in 1 to 9 | `prefix(t)` + じゅう + `r(ones)` (every accepted one) | 十四本 じゅうよんほん · 二十八本 にじゅうはっぽん, にじゅうはちほん |
| round tens, 10 geminates (k, s, h) | `prefix(t)` + the reading of 10 | 二十本 にじゅっぽん, にじっぽん · 三十分 さんじゅっぷん |
| round tens, plain counter | `prefix(t)` + じゅう + R | 二十枚 にじゅうまい · 三十円 さんじゅうえん |
| 100, k and h counters | ひゃっ + (the sound of R) | 百本 ひゃっぽん · 百回 ひゃっかい |
| 100, s and plain counters | ひゃく + R | 百冊 ひゃくさつ · 百歳 ひゃくさい · 百円 ひゃくえん |
| 円 hundreds | 200 にひゃく, 300 さんびゃく, 400 よんひゃく, 500 ごひゃく, 600 ろっぴゃく, 700 ななひゃく, 800 はっぴゃく, 900 きゅうひゃく + えん | 三百円 さんびゃくえん |
| 円 thousands | 千 せん, 二千 にせん, 三千 さんぜん, 四千 よんせん, 五千 ごせん, 六千 ろくせん, 七千 ななせん, 八千 はっせん, 九千 きゅうせん + えん; 万 いちまん | 三千円 さんぜんえん · 一万円 いちまんえん |

Authored exceptions: 二十歳 はたち; 二十日 はつか; 日 (date) 11 to 31 and 日間 11 to 31 as a table; 人 compounds
with 1 and 2 (じゅういちにん, じゅうににん); 月 4, 7, 9 (しがつ, しちがつ, くがつ).

Questions for the teacher: whether 100 geminates before the s row (this table says no: ひゃくさつ); the
accepted alternates for 80 and 800 (はちじゅう / はっぴゃく), 70 (ななじゅう / しちじゅう) and 7 or 9 in
compounds; 三十分 さんじゅっぷん and さんじっぷん; 十四日 じゅうよっか and 二十四日 にじゅうよっか; and whether a
counter list should ever include 一千 (いっせん).

## As built (stage 1)

What changed from the design while building the pipeline and data, and what has not been checked. The plan is
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-26-counters-big-numbers-stage-1.md`.

- **Bigger numbers are opt-in.** `extent` defaults to 10, not 99: with 99 every existing counter, including
  the irregular ones, failed validation until its data was written, and nothing should change before a
  counter asks. The data sets `extent` on all 29 counters (つ 10, 時 24, 分 59, the two 日 and 日間 31, 月 12,
  the rest 99) and `extra: [100]` on most; 円 also lists 200 to 900, 1000 to 9000 and 10000.
- **`compound`.** An irregular counter composes only through it; without it nothing is guessed (十一人 would
  have read じゅうひとり). 人, 時, both 日, 日間 and 月 carry one. An override beats composition (はたち,
  はつか, 羽's 百羽 ひゃっぱ).
- **Validation** covers `extent`, `extra` and `compound`, every override key, and a clear error for an
  irregular counter or an authored round number with nothing written. The counted-run check now reads compound
  numerals (十五分, 二十本, 三百円); a number the counter has no key for (百五十本) is skipped.
- **The numbers the data holds:** 11 to 99 for regular counters, 100, and 円's round numbers; 月 is the new
  counter (12 months, しがつ, しちがつ, くがつ). The generated tables were read against the appendix, and the
  app is unchanged: the 分 page shows the same 1 to 10 strip.
- **Preferred tens prefixes only** compose (にじゅう, ななじゅう); しちじゅう is not offered.

Checked: the unit tests (hand-written tables for one counter per sound class), the real-data tests for 27
readings from the appendix, the size budget (199,366 bytes), the unchanged core numbers, and one counter page
in the simulator with the new data.

**Not checked: the composition rules themselves.** They are mine and unreviewed. A teacher must rule on the
appendix questions before this data merges (100 before the s row, alternates for 70, 80 and 800, 三十分,
二十四日). The owner signs the data on the feature branch. Stages 2 and 3 (the stepper, Compare ranges,
search and the quiz) are not built, and the new numbers are not shown anywhere yet.

## As built (stage 2)

The app now shows the numbers above 10. The plan is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-counters-stage-2.md`.

- **Model.** `Counter` decodes `extent` and `extra` (old data: 10 and none). `CounterNumber.kanji` covers 1 to
  10000 (十五, 二十, 百, 三千, 一万). `Counter.extendedNumbers` lists the numbers above 10 that have readings;
  `Counter.step(_:by:)` moves through them, so the stepper cannot ask for a number the data lacks (円 steps
  99, 100, 200 and so on). `CounterNumber.isExtended` is the one place that says a number is unreviewed.
- **Counter page.** A stepper (−10, −1, the number, +1, +10) under the 1 to 10 strip, hidden for a counter with
  nothing above 10. The reading block shows "unreviewed" under the written form for any number above 10, and
  VoiceOver says it too.
- **Compare tables.** A segmented picker (1–10, 11–20, 30–90, 100+) appears when some counter in the group has
  numbers above 10, with "unreviewed" under it for every range but 1–10. A counter without a number shows a
  dash. Rows above 10 are 1.7 times taller because the longer readings wrap in three columns.
- **Search.** 15分, 十五分 and じゅうごふん find the same form; digits turn into kanji up to 10000; a bare
  じゅう, さんじゅう or ひゃく matches nothing, as a bare さん already did.

**Changed from the design.** The tens range lists only the round tens (30 to 90), so 21 to 29 and the other
in-between numbers are on the counter page only. The quiz, examples and pitfalls (stage 3) are not built.

**Checked on screen** (iPhone 17 Pro simulator): the stepper and the unreviewed label on 羽, and the Drinks
table's 11–20 range. **Not checked:** the 30–90 and 100+ ranges and 円's steps on screen (covered by unit
tests), largest Dynamic Type, dark mode, iPad, macOS, and the widgets.

## As built (stage 3)

The plan is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-counters-stage-3.md`.

- **Bigger numbers quiz.** A new row in the counter quiz sheet. It reuses the `.counterReading` question kind, so
  quiz history, weak spots and "Practise missed" key by counter and number as before and nothing new is stored.
  `buildBiggerNumberQuestions` draws (counter, number) pairs from `Counter.extendedNumbers` without replacement,
  four times as likely for a number whose sound changed or for an irregular counter. Wrong choices are
  the generated `distractors`, then readings of nearby other numbers above 10. After a wrong answer the
  explanation names the pattern ("三十分 is さんじゅっぷん: the sound changes here, as it does at 1, 3, 4, …").
- **Mixed** gets a sample, a fifth of the round and at least one question, from Bigger numbers. *Read it* still
  asks only 1 to 10 and 何.
- **Weak spots needed a fix the design did not foresee.** It silently dropped any pair whose number key was above
  10, so a missed 三十分 would never have come back. It now keeps every valid key, ordered 1 to 10, 何, then by value.
- **Unreviewed.** A question about a number above 10 shows the same small "unreviewed" label as the counter page.
- **Content.** Examples and pitfalls for 三十分 and 四十分 (分), 二十日 and 十四日 (日, date: はつか, じゅうよっか),
  and 三百円, 六百円, 八百円 and the thousands (円). 二十歳 and 十二月 already had theirs. Furigana for the new counted
  runs is in `furigana.json`.

**Changed from the design.** 日 (days) and 日間 got no new content: their pitfalls already cover 日 against 日間.

**Checked on screen** (iPhone 17 simulator): the sheet row and a Bigger numbers round (六十五着 with the label
and four real readings as choices). **Not checked:** the explanation after a wrong answer on screen, Mixed and
Weak spots with a bigger number on screen (covered by unit tests), largest Dynamic Type, dark mode.

**Needs the owner:** `data/` changed (counters 1.12.0, furigana 1.17.0), so `data/manifest.sig` must be re-signed.
