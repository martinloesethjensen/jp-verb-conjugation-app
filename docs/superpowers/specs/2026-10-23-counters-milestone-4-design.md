# Counters milestone 4: durations, more counters, 何 questions

Status: drafted 2026-10-23 from issue 124 and the content-gap review. The user asked to begin without a
question round, so every choice below is mine; the owner reviews the result. Content is unreviewed until
a teacher or native speaker checks it (issue 124 is labelled `teacher-review`).

## Problem

A learner who has finished the 18 N5 counters cannot yet say how long something took (3時間, 2週間, 3ヶ月,
5年間), has no counter for shoes (足), clothes (着), large animals (頭), birds (羽) or order (番), and the
quiz never asks the common question form (何人ですか, 何時間ですか). The 時 / 時間 and 日 / 日間 contrast, a
classic trap, is taught nowhere. 羽 is even named in the data as the primary counter for rabbits while
not existing.

## Goals

- Ten new counters, with readings generated by the existing pipeline where a class fits.
- A "when or how long?" comparison: point-in-time next to duration (時 / 時間, 日 / 日間, 年 / 年間).
- 何 + counter practice in the counter quiz.
- Everything validated by `update_data.py`, so a wrong reading cannot ship unnoticed.

## Non-goals

- Numbers above 10 and compound readings, so also months of the year (月, 1 to 12). Still the biggest
  gap, and its own project.
- Pitch accent, audio generation (no API calls here), Android and watchOS.
- Rewriting the existing 18 counters.

## Anti-goals

- Inventing rules in the app: readings stay in `counters.json`; the app derives nothing.
- Content that looks authoritative but is wrong. Every reading is checked by the generator; preferred
  readings follow the existing counters' conventions (4 as よ, 7 as なな).

Each gets 3 to 5 things, 2 or 3 examples and at least one pitfall, like the existing counters. Pitfalls
include: 時 is a clock time and 時間 a length (7時 しちじ vs 7時間 ななじかん), 日 vs 日間, 4時間 よじかん,
9時間 くじかん, ヶ月 may be written ヵ月, か月 or カ月 (all かげつ), 8週間 はっしゅうかん, 3足 さんぞく
(voiced), 3羽 さんば and 6羽 ろっぱ, 羽 also counts rabbits (but 匹 is heard).

`related`: each duration links to its point-in-time counter (時間 ↔ 時, 日間 ↔ 日, 年間 ↔ 年) and the
other durations; 羽 ↔ 匹 ↔ 頭; 足 ↔ 着. 匹's rabbit thing stays `acceptable` and 羽 lists うさぎ as `primary`.

## Groups and home sections

- `groups` (quiz distractors, search, routes): add `durations` ("How long": the five durations),
  `clothes` ("Clothes and shoes": 着, 足), `order` ("Order": 番); add 羽 and 頭 to `animals`.
- `sections` (home list): People & animals gains 羽 and 頭. New: **How long** (the five durations),
  **Clothes & shoes** (着, 足), and **When or how long?** (時, 時間, 日 (date), 日間, 年, 年間), the
  comparison. 番 joins Money & counting events. A counter may be in two sections (as 本 and つ are).

## The counted-run check

`check_counted_runs` reads 三時 inside 三時間 as a wrong reading of 時. It must not treat a numeral plus
counter followed by 間 as the shorter counter (unless the counter itself ends in 間). Examples keep their
counted runs in `furigana.json`, which the pipeline already requires.

## 何 questions

Each counter may carry `questions`: `[{"jp": "教室に何人いますか。", "en": "How many people are in the
classroom?"}]`. `jp` must contain 何 plus the counter's own written form (validated, and its reading is
checked like any counted run). About one per counter, written to separate confusable counters (何時 vs
何時間, 何日 vs 何日間).

New quiz kind `counterAsk` ("Ask it"): the English question is the prompt; the choices are four 何〜
phrases (何人, 何台, 何本, …), wrong ones from counters sharing a group first. Choosing the right one shows
the Japanese sentence and the reading of the 何 phrase. Attempts count toward streaks and totals; they do
not feed Weak spots (as *Which counter?*). Old app builds skip attempts of an unknown kind. The quiz sheet
gains an "Ask it" row; Mixed includes it.

## Constraints

- Data changes are re-signed by the owner before merge (`make sign-commit`); the signing happens on the
  feature branch, not on `main`.
- `questions` and the new fields are optional in decoding, so an old cache and old builds keep working.
- The existing `RealCountersDataTests` expectations (18 counters, five sections) are updated, not loosened.
