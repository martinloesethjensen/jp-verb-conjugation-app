# Counters milestone 4: durations, more counters, 何 questions

Status: drafted 2026-10-23 from issue 124 and the content-gap review. The user asked to begin without a
question round, so every choice below is mine; the owner reviews the result. Content is unreviewed until
a teacher or native speaker checks it (issue 124 is labelled `teacher-review`).

## Problem

A learner who has finished the 18 N5 counters cannot yet say how long something took (3時間, 2週間, 3ヶ月,
5年間), has no counter for shoes (足), clothes (着), large animals (頭), birds (羽) or order (番), and the
quiz never asks the common question form (何人ですか, 何時間ですか). The 時 / 時間 and 日 / 日間 contrast, a
classic trap, is taught nowhere. 羽 is even named in the data as the primary counter for rabbits while
not existing.

## Goals

- Ten new counters, with readings generated by the existing pipeline where a class fits.
- A "when or how long?" comparison: point-in-time next to duration (時 / 時間, 日 / 日間, 年 / 年間).
- 何 + counter practice in the counter quiz.
- Everything validated by `update_data.py`, so a wrong reading cannot ship unnoticed.

## Non-goals

- Numbers above 10 and compound readings, so also months of the year (月, 1 to 12). Still the biggest
  gap, and its own project.
- Pitch accent, audio generation (no API calls here), Android and watchOS.
- Rewriting the existing 18 counters.

## Anti-goals

- Inventing rules in the app: readings stay in `counters.json`; the app derives nothing.
- Content that looks authoritative but is wrong. Every reading is checked by the generator; preferred
  readings follow the existing counters' conventions (4 as よ, 7 as なな).

Each gets 3 to 5 things, 2 or 3 examples and at least one pitfall, like the existing counters. Pitfalls
include: 時 is a clock time and 時間 a length (7時 しちじ vs 7時間 ななじかん), 日 vs 日間, 4時間 よじかん,
9時間 くじかん, ヶ月 may be written ヵ月, か月 or カ月 (all かげつ), 8週間 はっしゅうかん, 3足 さんぞく
(voiced), 3羽 さんば and 6羽 ろっぱ, 羽 also counts rabbits (but 匹 is heard).

`related`: each duration links to its point-in-time counter (時間 ↔ 時, 日間 ↔ 日, 年間 ↔ 年) and the
other durations; 羽 ↔ 匹 ↔ 頭; 足 ↔ 着. 匹's rabbit thing stays `acceptable` and 羽 lists うさぎ as `primary`.

## Groups and home sections

- `groups` (quiz distractors, search, routes): add `durations` ("How long": the five durations),
  `clothes` ("Clothes and shoes": 着, 足), `order` ("Order": 番); add 羽 and 頭 to `animals`.
- `sections` (home list): People & animals gains 羽 and 頭. New: **How long** (the five durations),
  **Clothes & shoes** (着, 足), and **When or how long?** (時, 時間, 日 (date), 日間, 年, 年間), the
  comparison. 番 joins Money & counting events. A counter may be in two sections (as 本 and つ are).

## The counted-run check

`check_counted_runs` reads 三時 inside 三時間 as a wrong reading of 時. It must not treat a numeral plus
counter followed by 間 as the shorter counter (unless the counter itself ends in 間). Examples keep their
counted runs in `furigana.json`, which the pipeline already requires.

## 何 questions

Each counter may carry `questions`: `[{"jp": "教室に何人いますか。", "en": "How many people are in the
classroom?"}]`. `jp` must contain 何 plus the counter's own written form (validated, and its reading is
checked like any counted run). About one per counter, written to separate confusable counters (何時 vs
何時間, 何日 vs 何日間).

New quiz kind `counterAsk` ("Ask it"): the English question is the prompt; the choices are four 何〜
phrases (何人, 何台, 何本, …), wrong ones from counters sharing a group first. Choosing the right one shows
the Japanese sentence and the reading of the 何 phrase. Attempts count toward streaks and totals; they do
not feed Weak spots (as *Which counter?*). Old app builds skip attempts of an unknown kind. The quiz sheet
gains an "Ask it" row; Mixed includes it.

## Constraints

- Data changes are re-signed by the owner before merge (`make sign-commit`); the signing happens on the
  feature branch, not on `main`.
- `questions` and the new fields are optional in decoding, so an old cache and old builds keep working.
- The existing `RealCountersDataTests` expectations (18 counters, five sections) are updated, not loosened.

## Left to the builder

Exact things, examples and pitfall wording within the constraints above; the choice of one or two
questions per counter; the "Ask it" copy.

## As built

What changed from the design while building it, and what has not been checked. The plan is
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-counters-milestone-4.md`.

- **The months counter is written か月, not ヶ月.** Furigana keys cannot hold ヶ, and 三ヶ月 would be spoken
  as さんヶげつ. The meaning line lists ヶ月 and カ月, so search still finds it, and a pitfall lists the four
  spellings. `furigana.json` gained 月 → げつ and the stems 一か, 三か, 六か, 八か, 十か, which means a lone 月
  in future text now reads げつ silently. 何か月 takes its own example `readings` (`何か` is なに elsewhere).
- **The comparison is a section.** "When or how long?" (時, 時間, 日 date, 日間, 年, 年間) is a home section whose
  Compare table sets each point in time beside its duration; the pitfalls and `related` links say it in words.
- **羽 stays class `plain` with overrides**, so the Sound patterns exceptions line now also names 時間, 年間
  and 羽. An existing test that expected two exceptions was updated, and the counts (28 counters, 17 groups,
  8 sections) too.
- **The validator** skips a counted run followed by 間 (三時間 is not a run of 時), checks the optional
  `questions` field, and reads each question's 何 phrase like any counted run. It caught 何人, 何本 and nine
  more that the dictionary would have read as なに….
- **Ask it** shows the English question, offers four 何 phrases (same-group counters first, never the same
  text twice), and always shows the Japanese sentence and the phrase's reading after answering. It has no
  speak button. Attempts count toward streaks and totals, not Weak spots.
- **An app-side check of counted-run ruby** (`RealCountersDataTests`) runs the Swift matcher on the real
  `furigana.json`. The seeded simulator run used the old published furigana and showed 七時 間 with あいだ over
  間; the test fails on the old dictionary and passes on the new one. **The new `furigana.json` must be
  published with the counters**, or the new examples show wrong ruby until it is.

Checked on the iPhone simulator (counters seeded from the local file, furigana not): the new home sections,
the When or how long? Compare table, the 時間 page, and an Ask it question with its feedback. Checked by
tests: every reading in the spec table, all counted runs in examples and questions, the 27 questions
building choices.

Not checked: Ask it with levels hidden, with VoiceOver, at the largest text size, a wrong answer, Mixed,
Weak spots, the new pages on iPad and macOS, and the ruby on screen with the new furigana published.

**Fixed after the review** (the data was signed and pushed before these; it needs signing again):
- The validator now reads pitfall headings and explanations for counted runs, not only examples. It found
  wrong ruby in them: 四時間 as よじあいだ, 八着 as はちつ, 十羽 as じゅうわ, and in three older counters 十分
  and 一日 read the wrong way beside text saying otherwise. Those three pitfalls were reworded, and
  `furigana.json` gained the missing runs, 日間, 週間, 年間 and the month names (三月 had shown さんげつ).
- A question must hold 何 plus its own counter and not 何 plus counter plus 間, so 何時間 can no longer be
  filed under 時. Two Ask it questions that two phrases answered (何日 and 何日間, 何年 and 何年間) were
  reworded to ask for a length.
- 十羽 also reads じゅうわ and 七週間 also しちしゅうかん; 部屋 was replaced by the platform under 番.
- The Swift ruby test now covers pitfall text, and a test pins the month names.

**For the teacher review** (the reviewer rated these doubtful, not wrong): 九年間 くねんかん and 四時間
よんじかん as accepted readings; whether 何羽 should show なんわ before なんば; 羽 for rabbits (今は匹 is the usual
counter); the JLPT levels of 年間 and 日間 (N5 may be generous); これは何円ですか versus いくらですか as a
question.

**The content is unreviewed.** Readings are generated and validated, but the things, example sentences,
pitfall wording and question sentences were written without a native speaker; issue 124 stays labelled
`teacher-review`. Still out of scope: numbers above 10 (and so months of the year), pitch accent, audio, and
the Android and watchOS counterparts of the new counters and Ask it.
