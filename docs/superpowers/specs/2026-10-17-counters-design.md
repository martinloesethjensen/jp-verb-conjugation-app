# Counters: design

Status: drafted in chat 2026-10-17, pending the owner's review.

## Problem

Learners know they need a counter (本 for pencils, 枚 for paper). What they get wrong is
the reading: さんぼん, not さんほん; いっぽん, not いちほん; ひとり, not いちにん. The sound
changes cluster on a few numbers (1, 3, 6, 8, 10 and 何) and follow a handful of patterns
by the counter's first sound, with a few fully irregular counters on top.

So the unit worth teaching and tracking is a **counter with a number** (3本 → さんぼん), not
the counter alone. The app has no counters today.

## Decisions

- **A fourth tab,** Counters, between Grammar and Word Bank. Counters are a reading
  system with their own lookup, not a grammar pattern.
- **About 15 N5 counters in milestone 1:** つ, 人, 本, 杯, 匹, 分, 個, 回, 階, 冊, 歳, 枚,
  台, 円, 時. Together they cover every sound class and the main irregulars, and are few
  enough to review by hand.
- **Readings are generated in the data pipeline,** like adjective forms: authors write a
  sound class and any exceptions, and `update_data.py` fills every reading. The app has no
  reading logic.
- **Numbers 1–10 and 何 only.** All the irregularity is there. Compound numbers (21本) are
  out of scope, so the app never composes readings.
- **The playground ships in milestone 1,** as a card on each counter page.
- **Quiz attempts stay in one table.** `QuizAttempt.verb` is renamed `subject` and holds a
  counter id for counter questions.
- **Three milestones,** each shipping on its own: 1 reference and playground, 2 counter
  quiz, 3 sound patterns and widget.

## Design

### Sound classes

Base numbers: いち に さん よん ご ろく なな はち きゅう じゅう, and なん for 何. The
classes say which of them change:

| Class | Counters (m1) | 1 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 何 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `plain` | 枚, 台, 円 | いち | さん | よん | ろく | はち | じゅう | なん |
| `k` | 個, 回, 階 | いっ | さん | よん | ろっ | はっ, はち | じゅっ, じっ | なん |
| `s` | 冊, 歳 | いっ | さん | よん | ろく | はっ | じゅっ, じっ | なん |
| `h-b` | 本, 杯, 匹 | いっ+p | さん+b | よん | ろっ+p | はっ+p, はち | じゅっ+p, じっ+p | なん+b |
| `h-p` | 分 | いっ+p | さん+p | よん+p | ろっ+p | はっ+p, はち | じゅっ+p, じっ+p | なん+p |
| `irregular` | つ, 人, 時 | written in full by the author | | | | | | |

- `+p` and `+b` turn the counter's は-row sound into ぱ or ば (ほん → ぽん, ぼん).
- 2, 5, 7 and 9 never change in these classes, and 7 is always なな.
- The first reading in a list is the preferred one. はちほん and じっぽん are accepted
  but never shown first.
- Exceptions inside a class are overrides: 円 4 is よえん, and 階 3 and 何 are さんがい
  and なんがい, with さんかい and なんかい accepted.
- `irregular` counters have no generated readings. Their `overrides` must cover all 11
  keys: つ (ひとつ … とお, 何 いくつ), 人 (ひとり, ふたり, よにん, しちにん as a second
  reading for 7), 時 (よじ, しちじ, くじ).

### Data

**`data/counters.json`** (new; hand-authored except `readings` and `changed`):

```json
{
  "version": "1.0.0",
  "description": "Japanese counters. readings and changed are generated by scripts/update_data.py.",
  "counters": [
    { "id": "hon", "counter": "本", "reading": "ほん", "meaning": "long, thin things",
      "jlpt": "N5", "sound": "h-b", "symbol": "pencil",
      "overrides": {},
      "readings": { "1": ["いっぽん"], "2": ["にほん"], "3": ["さんぼん"], "4": ["よんほん"],
                    "5": ["ごほん"], "6": ["ろっぽん"], "7": ["ななほん"],
                    "8": ["はっぽん", "はちほん"], "9": ["きゅうほん"],
                    "10": ["じゅっぽん", "じっぽん"], "what": ["なんぼん"] },
      "changed": ["1", "3", "6", "8", "10", "what"],
      "things": [ { "word": "鉛筆", "kana": "えんぴつ", "meaning": "pencil", "fit": "primary", "symbol": "pencil" },
                  { "word": "傘", "kana": "かさ", "meaning": "umbrella", "fit": "primary", "symbol": "umbrella" },
                  { "word": "ビール", "kana": "ビール", "meaning": "beer (bottles)", "fit": "primary", "symbol": "waterbottle" } ],
      "examples": [ { "jp": "ビールを二本ください。", "en": "Two bottles of beer, please." } ],
      "pitfalls": [ { "heading": "Not さんほん", "explanation": "…", "examples": [] } ],
      "related": ["hai", "hiki"] }
  ]
}
```

- `sound` is a class from the table above. `overrides` maps a key (`"1"`…`"10"`,
  `"what"`) to readings that replace the generated ones.
- `readings` is generated: the class's rule, then overrides. `changed` is generated too:
  the keys whose preferred reading is not the base number plus the counter's own reading.
  It is what the page and playground highlight, so the app does not decide what changed.
- `things` are plain nouns with no forms. `fit` is `primary`, `acceptable` (rabbits under
  匹 when 羽 is primary) or `casual` (the つ or 個 fallback). A thing may appear under
  several counters. `symbol` (an SF Symbol name) is optional and falls back to the
  counter's `symbol`.
- `examples` and `pitfalls` reuse the grammar shapes (`GrammarExample`, `GrammarPitfall`).
- `update_data.py` checks:
  - every counter has all 11 readings and every reading is hiragana
  - `sound` is known, and `irregular` counters override every key
  - override keys are valid
  - `related` ids resolve, and `fit` and `jlpt` values are known
  - `--check` fails when `readings` or `changed` are stale
- The furigana coverage check includes `counters.json`. Example sentences need their
  counted runs in `furigana.json` (二本 → にほん), because the dictionary on its own would
  read 本 as ほん.
- New manifest entry `counters` (version and sha256), signed like the others. A
  `CountersSyncService` and storage follow the `GrammarSyncService` pattern.

### VerbKit

- `Counter`, `CountedThing`, `CounterDataFile` and `CounterFit` models. Old app builds
  ignore the new manifest key.
- `CounterNumber`: `.n(1...10)` or `.what`, with the JSON key and the display form
  (三本, 何本).
- `Counter.readings(for:) -> [String]` and `Counter.isChanged(_:)` only read data.
- `CounterSearch`: matches counter, reading, meaning, thing word, kana and meaning, with
  romaji through the existing `Romaji`. A match on a thing returns its counters, primary
  first.
- `Route.counter(String)` and `verbtable://counter/<id>`. `RouteTarget` gains `.counter`.
- Ruby for readings cells comes from `counters.json`, never from `FuriganaDictionary`.
- Spotlight indexes counters and their things ("umbrella" → 本).
- Milestone 2:
  - `QuizAttempt.verb` → `subject`, with `@Attribute(originalName: "verb")` on the entity
    (lightweight migration).
  - New `QuizQuestionKind` cases: `counterChoice` (formID = the thing's word) and
    `counterReading` (formID = the number key).
  - A counter question builder and Weak spots for `counterReading` pairs.

### App

**Counters tab** (`textformat.123`), a `NavigationSplitView` like Grammar:

- **List:** grouped by level, filtered by the JLPT settings. Each row shows the counter
  big, its reading, its meaning and its symbol. Search uses `CounterSearch`, so typing
  "umbrella", "kasa" or 傘 finds 本 with "傘 · umbrella" shown as the reason.
- **Counter page,** in this order:
  1. Header: 本, ほん, "long, thin things", symbol, speaker.
  2. **Count them** (playground): a 1–10 stepper (and a 何 chip), a row of the thing's
     symbol repeated n times, and the reading below it, large, with 三本 small above.
     When the reading is in `changed`, the changed part is tinted and the cell gets a dot
     marker, so the change does not rely on colour alone. Changes animate (いっぽん →
     にほん → さんぼん). A thing picker swaps the symbol. Tapping the reading speaks it.
  3. **Readings:** a grid of 1–10 and 何 with `changed` cells marked. Tap to speak,
     long-press to copy (the `FormCell` behaviour). Second readings show small beneath.
  4. **What it counts:** thing chips, primary first. Tapping a chip shows its other
     counters ("rabbit: 羽, also 匹").
  5. Examples. 6. Watch out (`pitfalls`). 7. Related counters.
  8. Practise this counter (milestone 2).
- Speech reads the **kana** reading, never the kanji, so 一日 or 八本 can't come out wrong.
  `generate_audio.py` picks the readings up as texts.

**Counter quiz (milestone 2):** multiple choice, no timer, its own screen like Connections.

- *Which counter?* Show a thing (傘 + symbol), pick from four counters. Distractors come
  from other counters for similar-looking things.
- *Read it.* Show 三本, pick the reading. Distractors are the unchanged reading
  (さんほん), the neighbouring class's change (さんぽん) and a misplaced small っ
  (さっぽん). Any accepted reading counts as correct.
- A wrong answer names the pattern: "本 changes at 1, 3, 6, 8, 10: さんぼん".
- Attempts count toward streaks and totals. Counter Weak spots is a row on the quiz sheet
  when the Counters tab starts it.

**Milestone 3:**

- **Sound patterns:** one screen with a row per class, showing which numbers change.
  Tapping a column (6) highlights ろっ across the k and h classes.
- A **counter of the day** widget showing the readings grid. It opens via
  `verbtable://counter/<id>`.

### Content (milestone 1)

- The 15 counters above, each with 3–6 things, 2–3 examples and at least one pitfall.
  Pitfalls include: 4人 is よにん, 20歳 is はたち, 9時 is くじ, 3階 is さんがい, and
  "when unsure, つ works up to 10".
- Written by the assistant and unreviewed until the owner or a native speaker checks it.
  Every `data/` change is re-signed by the owner before merge.

## Milestones

1. **Reference and playground:** counters.json, generation and validation, sync, models,
   search, route, the Counters tab, the counter page with Count them.
2. **Counter quiz:** the `subject` rename, two question kinds, Weak spots for readings.
3. **Patterns and widget.**

## Testing

- **Python (`test_update_data.py`):** every class's generated table against the
  hand-written expected readings for one counter each (本, 分, 個, 冊, 枚); overrides
  replace generated keys; `changed` for each class; validation failures (missing key,
  unknown class, irregular without full overrides, katakana reading, bad `related`).
- **VerbKit:** decoding (including old manifests without `counters`); sync like grammar
  (manifest, signature, hash); `CounterSearch` on thing meaning, kana, romaji and
  counter; route URL round trip; milestone 2: the attempt migration keeps old rows,
  question building with all accepted readings correct, and distractors never equal to
  an accepted reading.
- **App:** simulator checks of the tab, search, counter page, playground animation and
  speech. iPad and macOS checks stay parked until the owner resumes them.

## Out of scope

- Numbers above 10 and compound readings (21本, 100本).
- Dates (ついたち, ふつか), months and durations (〜ヶ月, 〜週間). They are their own
  irregular system and could be a later milestone.
- A counter field on Word Bank entries.
- Recorded native audio and pitch accent.
- Typed answers in the quiz.
